Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

Katy's retail growth around Katy Mills Mall and LaCenterra at Cinco Ranch has brought a steady increase in commercial plumbing needs, from grease trap maintenance for new food service tenants to fixture repair for offices and retail spaces. Older commercial buildings and new construction both come with their own specific commercial plumbing considerations. A tenant moving into a space previously occupied by a different type of business often inherits plumbing infrastructure that was never sized for the new use, which is worth assessing before signing a lease rather than discovering it after opening.

Multiple Fixture Issues Across a Property

Diagnosis

How We Work With Businesses

A plumber assesses the property's actual plumbing needs, not a generic residential checklist, and provides an itemized scope for property managers or business owners who need that documentation for approval. Work is scheduled around your business hours where possible, early morning, evening, or overnight for larger jobs, to minimize disruption. For a multi-tenant property, we coordinate directly with property management so repairs and scheduled maintenance don't require the same conversation to happen separately for every unit.

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Keep grease traps on a regular service schedule rather than waiting for a backup.

Know where your property's main water and gas shutoffs are before an emergency happens.

Post emergency plumbing contact information where staff can find it quickly.

Confirm floor drains are checked during regular cleaning, not just when water starts pooling.
