Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

Mature trees in Katy's older neighborhoods send roots into aging clay sewer lines over time, which is the most common cause of a recurring clog that comes back a few weeks after snaking. Newer-construction homes in communities like Cane Island run modern PVC lines that resist root intrusion, but can still clog from grease or construction debris left behind during the move-in period. Either way, the fix depends on which of those two causes is actually at work, not just on which fixture happens to be backing up.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Slow-Draining Sink or Tub

Water backs up and drains slower than it used to, even after plunging.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped in the line trying to work around a partial blockage.

Multiple Fixtures Backing Up Together

Usually means the blockage is in a shared line, not a single fixture.

Sewage Odor Near a Drain

Often a sign of a blockage trapping waste further down the line.

Water Backing Up Into a Different Fixture

Running the washing machine and seeing water rise in a tub or shower is a classic sign of a shared-line blockage, not two separate problems.

Diagnosis

How We Clear It

For a straightforward clog, a hand-fed drain snake usually clears it in one visit. For a clog that keeps coming back, root intrusion, or heavy grease buildup, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full diameter of the pipe rather than just punching a hole through the blockage. A camera inspection can confirm the actual cause before recommending which approach fits, which matters most on an older Katy property where the sewer line's real condition isn't visible from the yard.

Options

Snaking or Hydro Jetting?

Snaking Usually Handles

  • A single clogged fixture
  • Hair, soap buildup, minor blockages
  • A clog with no history of recurring

Hydro Jetting Is Better For

  • A clog that keeps returning
  • Root intrusion in older clay lines
  • Grease buildup along the pipe walls

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Avoid chemical drain cleaners before we arrive. They can damage older pipes and make the actual cause harder to diagnose.

If sewage is backing up, stay out of the affected area rather than trying to clean it yourself.

Shut off the water to any fixture that's actively overflowing.

Note which fixtures are affected and in what order. It helps narrow down where the blockage actually is.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning at night?

Yes, our Katy dispatch line runs 24/7 for backups and overflows, including nights and weekends.

Will hydro jetting damage older pipes?

A plumber inspects the pipe material and condition first. Hydro jetting is calibrated to the line, not applied blind.

How do I know if it's a simple clog or something bigger?

If more than one fixture is affected at the same time, or the same drain keeps clogging within weeks, it's worth a camera inspection rather than repeated snaking.

Can I use a store-bought drain cleaner first?

Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipes and make the actual cause harder to diagnose. A mechanical snake is usually the safer first step.
