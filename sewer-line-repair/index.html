Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

Katy's older neighborhoods have mature trees whose roots find their way into aging clay and cast iron sewer lines through small cracks over decades, while the region's expansive clay soil can also shift and stress sewer lines regardless of a home's age. Newer PVC lines resist both issues far better, which is part of why sewer line problems skew toward Katy's more established neighborhoods.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Sewer Line Repair

Sewage Backing Up Into the Home

A serious sign requiring immediate attention, not a wait-and-see situation.

Multiple Drains Backing Up Together

Points to a main sewer line issue rather than an isolated fixture problem.

Sewage Odor in the Yard

Can indicate a break or crack in the line somewhere underground.

Unusually Lush Grass in One Yard Area

Sometimes a sign of a leaking sewer line acting as fertilizer for that section.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose and Repair It

A camera inspection confirms the exact cause and location before any digging starts, root intrusion, a cracked joint, or a fully collapsed section all need different repairs. Depending on what the camera shows, the fix is either a targeted spot repair, a trenchless lining option, or in severe cases, a full line replacement, each one completed to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standard adopted in Texas.

Options

Spot Repair, Trenchless Lining, or Full Replacement?

A Spot Repair or Lining Fits When

  • Damage is isolated to one section of the line
  • The rest of the pipe is in reasonable condition
  • A camera confirms a specific, limited problem

Full Replacement Is Needed When

  • The line has widespread damage or collapse
  • Multiple sections show significant deterioration
  • Repeated repairs haven't resolved recurring issues

What We Don't Cover

Scope, Honestly

We repair and replace residential sewer lines up to the property line. A municipal sewer main issue beyond your property line gets referred directly to the correct utility rather than treated as a repair we can actually perform ourselves at all.

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

If sewage is backing up, stay out of the affected area and avoid contact with the water.

Shut off water to any actively overflowing fixture.

Avoid running additional water (laundry, dishwasher) until the line is diagnosed.

Note which drains are affected and in what order; it helps narrow down the blockage location before a technician even arrives on site.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you diagnose sewer line damage without digging up my yard?

Yes, a camera inspection locates the exact problem first, which often allows for a trenchless repair without any full yard excavation at all.

How quickly can you respond to a sewage backup?

Our emergency line runs 24/7 for sewage backups, since they need immediate attention.

Is trenchless sewer repair available for older Katy homes?

Often yes, depending on the specific damage. A camera inspection confirms whether your situation qualifies.

How disruptive is sewer line repair to my yard or landscaping?

A trenchless option, when it qualifies, is far less disruptive than full excavation, which is always worth asking about directly before work begins.
