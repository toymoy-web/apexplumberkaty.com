Who We Are

Plumbing Built Around How Katy Actually Grew

Katy Apex Plumbing dispatches licensed plumbers across Katy, Cinco Ranch, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, and Brookshire, covering the real range of housing this part of West Houston actually has: decades-old homes near Katy's historic core, fast-growing new construction in communities like Cane Island and Cross Creek Ranch, and everything in between.

That range matters because the plumbing itself is genuinely different depending on a home's age and location, not just its address. A plumber dispatched to an older Katy neighborhood expects different pipe materials and failure patterns than one dispatched to a home built in the last five years, and we built our approach around that real distinction rather than treating every call the same way.

What We Actually Do

The Full Range of Services

From an emergency water heater failure at midnight to a scheduled water softener installation, our plumbers handle the full range of residential plumbing across Katy, plus commercial work for the area's growing retail corridors. Every service we offer is listed on our services page, along with what each one actually involves and what it typically costs.

We don't limit ourselves to the most common calls. Slab leak detection, sewer camera inspection, and whole-home repiping all come up regularly given Katy's mix of decades-old homes and newer construction, and we've built our team around handling that full range rather than referring out anything beyond the basics.

Our Standard

Every visit follows the same standard regardless of which service you need: a real inspection before any work starts, a flat price you approve before the plumber proceeds, and honest communication about what's actually wrong rather than an upsell. Emergency Response

24/7 Really Means 24/7

Our emergency line is answered around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays, because plumbing emergencies don't wait for business hours. A burst pipe at 2am or a sewage backup on a Sunday gets the same immediate response as a call during a Tuesday afternoon, dispatched to a plumber who's actually equipped for the specific emergency you're describing.

Two Housing Eras, One Standard

Older Homes and New Construction, Treated Differently on Purpose

A house near Katy's historic district and a home in a newer community like Cane Island genuinely need different diagnostic approaches, even for the same symptom. Older homes are more likely to be running original galvanized or copper supply lines that have had decades of exposure to this region's clay soil, while newer construction is more likely to have an installation issue than an age-related one. Our plumbers are trained to recognize which profile a call is coming from rather than applying a single generic checklist to every address.
