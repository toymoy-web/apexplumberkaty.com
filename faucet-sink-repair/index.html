Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

The mineral content in West Houston's water wears out faucet cartridges and valve seats faster than a softer-water market would, which is why a fixture that's only a few years old can already be dripping. Older homes near Katy's historic district may still be running original fixtures from construction, which are more likely to need full replacement than a repair. New-construction homes aren't immune either. A cheaper builder-grade fixture can wear out just as fast as an old one if it was never a high-quality part to begin with.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Faucet & Sink Repair

A Persistent Drip

Continues even with the handle fully closed.

Low Flow or Pressure

Often mineral buildup restricting flow inside the fixture, not the home's main line.

Handle Hard to Turn

A sign the internal cartridge or valve is wearing out.

Leaking Under the Sink

Usually a supply line connection or the shutoff valve itself, not the faucet.

Water Stains or Warping Under the Cabinet

A slow, unnoticed drip under the sink can warp cabinet wood long before it's visible from outside.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose It

A plumber identifies the fixture's brand and cartridge type first, since parts availability varies widely by manufacturer. From there it's a test of the cartridge or valve, a check of the supply line connections, and a check of the shutoff valves under the sink, since a failing shutoff is often mistaken for a faucet problem. On an older fixture, the plumber also checks whether the mounting itself has loosened, which can cause a leak that looks like a cartridge problem but isn't.

Options

Repair or Replace the Fixture?

Repair Usually Makes Sense When

  • The fixture is a well-known brand with parts still available
  • The body of the fixture shows no corrosion
  • This is the fixture's first repair

Replacement Usually Makes Sense When

  • The fixture is original to an older home
  • Parts for the brand are discontinued
  • This is a repeat repair on the same fixture, which usually means the part is failing again rather than being a one-time fix

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Turn off the fixture's local shutoff valve under the sink if it's actively leaking. You don't need to shut off the whole house.

If you can't find or reach the local valve, the home's main shutoff works as a temporary fallback.

Keep a bucket or towel under the leak while you wait to limit water damage to the cabinet.

Note the fixture's brand if it's visible. It speeds up parts availability on the visit.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you match my existing faucet brand?

In most cases yes. If the exact model is discontinued, a plumber can recommend a comparable replacement.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing right away?

Yes. Beyond the water cost, a worn cartridge can fail further and turn into a bigger leak.

Do you fix leaking supply lines under the sink?

Yes, that's one of the most common calls alongside faucet repair itself.

Can you install a new sink, not just repair a faucet?

Yes, including connecting the new sink's drain and supply lines to the existing plumbing, and confirming the new fixture matches your countertop's cutout.
