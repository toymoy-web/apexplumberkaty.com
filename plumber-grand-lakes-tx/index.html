Plumber in Grand Lakes, Katy, TX

Grand Lakes is a 1,250-acre planned community in Fort Bend County with 27 man-made lakes, built out mostly between 1998 and 2008. That puts most homes here at roughly 18 to 28 years old, the age when water heaters, supply lines and fixtures start asking for attention.

Where It Sits

Between Cinco Ranch and the Westpark Tollway

Grand Lakes sits south of Cinco Ranch and north of the Westpark Tollway. Phases one through three lie east of the Grand Parkway (SH 99), phase four is on the west side, Fry Road forms the eastern edge and Peek Road lies further west. Interstate 10 is a short drive to the north.

The community carries a Katy mailing address, but it sits in Houston's extraterritorial jurisdiction rather than inside the City of Katy. Water, sewer and drainage come from Municipal Utility Districts, and each district sets its own rate, so two neighbors can have different utility bills. Grand Lakes MUD 1 is one example.

Housing Age

Homes in Their Second and Third Decades

A tank water heater installed when a Grand Lakes home was new is well past the 8 to 12 years such units commonly last. Many homes have likely replaced one already, and a second replacement is not far off for some. Toilet fill valves, faucet cartridges and the flexible supply lines under sinks wear out on a similar schedule.

None of that means a particular house has a problem. It means a plumber should look at the age of each component before guessing, and the age of the home is a fair starting point for that conversation. Repairs and installations follow the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Texas.

Lakes and Leaks

Telling Water Features From Plumbing Leaks

With 27 lakes and a drainage system built around them, a damp patch of lawn or a wet edge along a slab does not automatically point to a pipe. A rising water bill with no visible cause is a better signal. Electronic leak detection and a pressure test separate a plumbing leak from ordinary drainage without opening walls or digging up the yard.

Many homes in this part of the Houston area sit on concrete slabs, so a leak in a line under the floor can go unnoticed for a while. That is why a sudden bill increase deserves a call, not a wait-and-see.

Signs to Watch For

Common Plumbing Signs in Grand Lakes

Water Bill Climbs With No Cause

A steady increase while usage stays the same is the most common early sign of a hidden leak.

Water Heater Past 10 Years

Rust-colored water, popping noises or moisture at the base of the tank are reasons to have it checked.

Slow Drains in Several Fixtures

One slow drain is usually local. Several at once can point to the main line.

Warm Spot on a Floor

A warm area on a slab floor can indicate a leaking hot water line beneath it.

FAQs

Questions From Grand Lakes

Is Grand Lakes inside the City of Katy?

Grand Lakes has a Katy mailing address but sits in Houston's extraterritorial jurisdiction and is served by Municipal Utility Districts. That does not change how a plumbing repair works. It does affect who handles the water main and public sewer outside your property line versus the pipes inside it.

Do you cover both sides of the Grand Parkway?

Yes. We dispatch to the phases east of the Grand Parkway and to the newer phase on the west side.

Should I repair or replace an older water heater?

It depends on the unit's age, whether the tank itself is leaking and how much a repair would cost compared with a new unit. A plumber can look at the tank and give you both options.

Can you tell a lake-related damp spot from a plumbing leak?

Often yes. Leak detection and a pressure test on the supply lines help separate the two, and a plumber can do that without digging first.
