Toilet Repair & Installation in Katy, TX

A running, leaking, or clogged toilet is one of the most common calls our Katy plumbers take, and most causes come down to a small number of inexpensive parts. We repair existing toilets and install new ones, including full replacements for older units and upgrades to modern low-flow toilet models.

Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

West Houston's mineral-heavy water wears out flapper valves and fill valves faster than a softer-water market would, which is why a toilet that's only a few years old can already be running. Older homes near Katy's historic core may still have original toilets from construction, which use significantly more water per flush than a modern low-flow model.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Toilet Repair & Installation

Continuously Running Water

Almost always a worn flapper valve failing to seal properly.

Water Pooling at the Base

Can indicate a failing wax ring seal between the toilet and drain flange.

Frequent Clogging

Sometimes a low-flow model with a weak flush, sometimes a partial line blockage.

Rocking or Unstable Toilet

Usually a loosened mounting or a failing wax ring, worth fixing before it leaks.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose It

For a running toilet, a plumber checks the flapper valve and fill valve first, since those two parts cause the large majority of cases. For water at the base, the wax ring and mounting bolts get inspected, since a failing seal there can let water and eventually sewage leak underneath with every flush if left alone.

Options

Repair or Full Replacement?

Repair Usually Makes Sense When

  • The toilet is a relatively recent model
  • The issue is a single part: flapper, fill valve, or wax ring
  • This is the toilet's first repair

Replacement Usually Makes Sense When

  • The toilet is original to an older home
  • This is a second or third repair on the same unit
  • You want to upgrade to a modern low-flow model

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Turn off the toilet's own shutoff valve at the wall if it's actively leaking; you don't need to shut off the whole house.

Avoid using chemical toilet bowl cleaners with drop-in tablets if the toilet is already having issues; they can accelerate flapper wear.

Don't keep flushing a clogged toilet repeatedly; it risks an overflow.

Note whether the issue is running, clogging, or leaking at the base; that detail speeds up the diagnosis and helps the plumber bring the right parts.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you match my existing toilet if I just need a repair part?

In most cases yes; if the exact model is discontinued, a plumber can recommend a comparable replacement part instead.

How long does a toilet installation take?

Most replacements are completed within an hour or two, including removal and proper disposal of the old unit.

Is a running toilet worth fixing right away?

Yes. It wastes real water every single day it runs and can indicate a part that's about to fail further.

Can you install a comfort-height or accessible toilet?

Yes, both are common upgrade requests and are straightforward to install during a standard toilet replacement service visit.
