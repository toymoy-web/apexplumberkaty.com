Running Out of Hot Water or Getting Lukewarm Water From a Tankless Unit?

A tankless unit that used to deliver endless hot water but now runs lukewarm, cuts out mid-shower, or throws an error code on the display is almost always a descaling issue, mineral buildup inside the heat exchanger restricting flow and throwing off the unit's temperature sensors, not a sign the whole unit is failing.

Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

West Houston's water carries enough mineral content that tankless heat exchangers scale up faster here than in a soft-water market, which is why a unit that's never been flushed on schedule tends to show symptoms sooner in Katy than the manufacturer's general guidance assumes.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Tankless Water Heater Service

Lukewarm or Inconsistent Water

Temperature drifts instead of holding steady through a shower.

Error Codes on the Display

Modern tankless units show a specific code, worth reading off before the plumber arrives.

Popping or Rattling Sounds

Often scale breaking loose inside the heat exchanger.

Reduced Flow at Multiple Fixtures

A sign the restriction is inside the unit itself, not a single fixture.

Diagnosis

How Service Works

A plumber connects to the unit's isolation valves and circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger, checks the venting and gas supply, and tests the flow sensor and igniter. Units that have never had isolation valves installed get those added first, since there's no way to flush a unit properly without them.

Options

Descale Service or Full Replacement?

Descale/Repair Usually Makes Sense When

  • The unit has never been flushed on the manufacturer's schedule
  • Symptoms are inconsistent temperature or an error code
  • The unit is under 8 years old

Replacement Usually Makes Sense When

  • The heat exchanger itself has failed or is cracked
  • Repeated descaling hasn't resolved the symptoms
  • You're upsizing for a larger household

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

If you smell gas near the unit, leave the house and call your gas utility before calling us.

Turn off the unit at its power source if it's throwing sparks or the area around it is wet.

Write down any error code shown on the display. It speeds up the phone diagnosis.

Don't attempt to open the unit's casing yourself. The heat exchanger and gas components inside aren't a DIY repair.

FAQs

Common Questions

Why does my tankless water heater need to be flushed?

Mineral content in Katy's water builds up inside the heat exchanger over time, and flushing it out is standard maintenance, not a sign of a defect.

How often should a tankless unit be serviced?

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in a market with West Houston's water hardness.

Can a tankless unit be repaired instead of replaced?

In most cases, yes. Full replacement is usually reserved for a cracked heat exchanger or a unit that's reached the end of its practical life.

Does a tankless unit need special venting?

Most do, and a plumber checks the existing venting during service since it's a common source of error codes if it was installed incorrectly, especially on a unit converted from a tank setup where the original venting was never resized for the new, higher-demand unit.
