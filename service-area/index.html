Counties Covered

Harris, Fort Bend, and Waller

Katy itself sits across three counties, and our coverage follows the real geography rather than stopping at a single county line. Cinco Ranch and Katy proper span Harris and Fort Bend, Fulshear and Richmond and Sugar Land sit in Fort Bend, and Brookshire is in Waller County. The same dispatched plumbers and the same upfront pricing apply no matter which county a call comes from.

Why Coverage Varies by Town

Real Differences, Not Just Distance

The reason each city gets its own page instead of one generic coverage map is that the plumbing genuinely differs by town. Fulshear is almost entirely new construction, with new-build warranty issues far more common than aging pipe. Richmond and Brookshire both have real historic cores where original fixtures and older pipe materials are still in daily use. Cinco Ranch and Sugar Land sit in between, large enough that they contain both older, established sections and newer master-planned ones inside the same city limits.

How Coverage Gets Decided

Distance and Density, Not a Hard Boundary

Coverage isn't drawn as a strict radius on a map. It follows where Katy's real communities actually sit, along the Grand Parkway, I-10, and the Westpark Tollway corridors that connect Katy to Cinco Ranch, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, and Brookshire. A property just outside one of these named towns but still along those same corridors is very likely still inside our dispatch radius, even if it isn't its own listed city.

Emergency vs. Scheduled Coverage

Same Coverage, Different Response Time

Every city on this page gets the same 24/7 emergency line and the same upfront pricing. The only real difference between towns is response time on a non-emergency call, since a plumber already working in Sugar Land can usually reach a nearby Richmond address faster than one dispatched from across the county. A dispatcher accounts for that automatically when scheduling, so you don't need to factor it in yourself.

Choosing the Right City Page

Why Each Town Gets Its Own Page

If you're comparing the individual city pages, the fastest way to pick the right one is by housing type rather than distance. A home built in the last five to ten years in Cross Creek Ranch, Jordan Ranch, Cane Island, or Aliana behaves more like the other new-construction pages on this site than like an older neighborhood a few miles closer to Katy's center. A pre-1990s home near a historic downtown, whether that's Richmond or Brookshire, shares more in common with each other than with a same-city new-build subdivision. Reading the page for your town's actual housing era, not just its name, gives a better sense of what a service call there tends to involve, and it's the same logic a dispatcher applies when deciding which plumber to send.
