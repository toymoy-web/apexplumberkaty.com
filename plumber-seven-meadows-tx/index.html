Where It Sits

A Large Community Made of Smaller Neighborhoods

Seven Meadows is built around trails and a central park, with homes typically running 2,200 to 4,000 square feet on lots of 7,000 to 12,000 square feet. It neighbors Grand Lakes and sits close to Cinco Ranch, though each is its own community with its own name.

Utility service comes through Fort Bend County MUD 34 and MUD 35. Not every parcel falls in both districts, and the Fort Bend Central Appraisal District website shows which one applies to a given address. It is worth knowing, because the district handles the public water and sewer lines while the pipes inside your property are yours.

Housing Age

Two-Story Homes About 20 Years On

Homes built in the early 2000s are now old enough that builder-grade faucets, toilet components and water heaters are reaching replacement age. A house of this size often has a bathroom upstairs over a living area, which matters because a slow leak there tends to show up first as a stain on the ceiling below instead of a puddle.

Larger homes also put more demand on the water heater. If hot water runs out quickly, the cause may be a failing element, sediment in the tank or a unit that was sized for a smaller household. A tankless water heater is one option to discuss, and a plumber can explain what a swap would involve in a house of this age.

Practical Guidance

What to Do When You Find a Ceiling Stain

A brown or yellow ring on a ceiling needs attention even if it dries. Shut off water to the fixture above if you can, place a bucket if it is dripping, and photograph the stain for your records. Then call so a plumber can trace the source before it spreads into insulation or drywall.

Keep in mind that not every stain is a supply line. Condensation from an air handler or a roof leak can look the same, and a plumber can rule plumbing in or out quickly. Repairs and installations follow the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Texas.

FAQs

Questions From Seven Meadows

How do I find out which MUD serves my Seven Meadows address?

The Fort Bend Central Appraisal District website lists the districts attached to a property, and your tax bill shows them too. Seven Meadows participates in MUD 34 and MUD 35, though not every parcel is in both.

Is Seven Meadows part of Cinco Ranch?

No. It is its own community with its own name and neighborhoods, though it sits near Cinco Ranch and Grand Lakes. We also serve those communities and have a separate page for Cinco Ranch.

Why is there a stain on my ceiling after a bathroom upstairs was used?

A slow leak in a drain, supply line or shower pan can reach the ceiling below before anything shows on the floor above. A plumber can trace it and tell you whether plumbing is the cause.

Can you help with a water heater that no longer keeps up?

Yes. We can check the unit's age, condition and sizing and talk through repair, replacement or a tankless option.
