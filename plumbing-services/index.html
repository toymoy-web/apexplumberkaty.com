How Pricing Works

Upfront Pricing, No Guessing

Every service on this page follows the same pricing approach. A plumber inspects the actual problem first, gives a flat price before any work starts, and only proceeds once you approve it. None of the ranges on our individual service pages are a final quote; they're a starting point so you know roughly what to expect before a technician is dispatched to your home.

Coverage Area

Where We Dispatch

We cover Katy proper along with Cinco Ranch, Fulshear, Richmond, Sugar Land, and Brookshire, spanning the three counties Katy sits across: Harris, Fort Bend, and Waller. Whether the job is in an older home near Katy's historic district or a newer build in a community like Cane Island, the same dispatched plumbers and the same upfront pricing apply across the whole coverage area.

Emergency vs. Scheduled Service

Which Kind of Call Is This?

Not every plumbing problem needs the emergency line. A burst pipe, no hot water in a house with young kids or elderly residents, or a sewage backup are the kind of calls that go straight into 24/7 dispatch. A dripping faucet, a disposal that's been humming for a day, or planning a future tankless install can usually wait for the next available scheduled slot, which tends to cost less than a true emergency call-out. If you're not sure which category your problem falls into, call anyway; the dispatcher sorts it, you don't have to guess.

Two Housing Eras, One Service Standard

Older Katy Homes and New Construction

A house near Katy's historic district and a home three miles north in Cane Island can need genuinely different diagnostic approaches even for the same symptom. Older plumbing runs different materials and has had decades to settle with the clay soil underneath it, while a newer system is more likely to have a manufacturing or installation issue than an age-related one. Every service on this page accounts for that difference rather than treating every house in Katy the same way.

Getting the Right Plumber Dispatched

What Happens When You Call

Describe the problem when you call, and the dispatcher matches it to the right service rather than sending a generalist to guess on-site. A water heater that's stopped heating gets someone equipped for that specific repair, not a plumber who then has to reschedule once the actual cause turns out to be something else. That matching happens before anyone leaves for your address, which is part of why most calls in Katy get resolved in a single visit, and it's also why describing the actual symptom on the phone, rather than a guess at the cause, helps the dispatcher route it correctly the first time. If you're genuinely unsure what's wrong, that's fine too; a dispatcher would rather ask a couple of questions upfront than send the wrong plumber out to your home.
