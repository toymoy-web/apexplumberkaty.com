What Brookshire Calls About Most

Well and Septic-Adjacent Properties, and Older Fixtures

Properties on the edge of Brookshire and toward neighboring Pattison are more likely to be on well water or septic than anywhere closer to Katy's core, which changes the diagnostic approach for a water pressure or drainage complaint. Inside town, the most common call is straightforward age-related fixture and water heater repair.

Neighborhoods

Downtown Brookshire and the I-10 Corridor

Brookshire's small downtown along US-90 reflects its agricultural history, while growth closer to Interstate 10 has brought some newer construction into the mix. Nearby Pattison, a few miles northwest, shares a similar rural character and falls within the same general service area.

Local History

A Farming Town Since the 1830s

Brookshire was named for Captain Nathen Brookshire, who received the land as part of Stephen F. Austin's fifth colony in 1835, and the town grew into a real agricultural shipping point for cotton, rice, and pecans once the railroad arrived. That long agricultural history is part of why Brookshire's housing stock skews older and more varied than the master-planned communities closer to Katy, and why a plumber working here needs to be comfortable with a wider range of home ages, pipe materials, and fixture styles in a single day than a route through a single newer subdivision would ever require, and it is why arriving with a broad set of spare parts on the truck matters more here than it does elsewhere.

Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Brookshire

Brookshire's older, more varied housing stock and rural edge-of-town properties both need the same immediate response to a burst pipe, whether the home runs city water or is on a private well.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source before anything else, if you know where your shutoff is located.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

Cut electricity at the breaker to any area with standing water before anyone walks through it.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the active leak or flooding.
