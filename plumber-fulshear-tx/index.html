What Fulshear Calls About Most

New-Construction Plumbing Issues, Not Aging Pipe

Because Fulshear's growth has been so recent and so fast, the most common calls here are installation and warranty-era issues rather than the corroded galvanized pipe or root-intruded clay sewer lines that show up in older Houston-area towns. A new water heater that was never sized correctly for the household, or a fixture that wasn't properly sealed during a builder-grade install, are the more typical Fulshear call.

Housing Era

Cross Creek Ranch, Jordan Ranch, and the New-Build Wave

Cross Creek Ranch alone covers roughly 3,200 acres, and Jordan Ranch another 1,350, both still building out new sections. That means a huge share of Fulshear's housing stock is under 10 years old, running PEX supply lines and modern fixtures that are less prone to the age-related failures common elsewhere in West Houston, but more prone to installation defects that show up in the first few years.

Growth Rate

One of the Fastest-Growing Cities in the Country

Fulshear's population grew by more than 750 percent between 2010 and 2017 alone, and it's continued adding homes every year since. That pace of growth means the area's plumbing infrastructure, water pressure, drainage capacity, utility connections, is being built and tested in real time rather than settled decades ago, which is part of why new-construction issues show up here more than aging infrastructure does. New installations still have to meet the same Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards as anywhere else in Texas, growth doesn't change the code a builder's plumber has to meet.

Neighborhoods

Where the Growth Is Concentrated

Beyond Cross Creek Ranch and Jordan Ranch, Tamarron, Polo Ranch, Pecan Ridge, and Fulshear Lakes are all active new-construction communities inside the 77441 ZIP code, with Jordan Ranch spilling into the neighboring 77423. A retail corridor anchored by H-E-B along the Texas Heritage Parkway has grown alongside the housing, which is a useful landmark for locating a service call in a community that's still filling in street by street.

Storm Season

New Drainage Systems, Same Gulf Coast Storms

New-construction drainage and sump systems are generally better engineered than an older neighborhood's original infrastructure, but they're not exempt from Gulf Coast storm season. A sump pump that's never been tested under real load is a common gap in a home that's only been through one or two wet seasons.

Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Fulshear

Fulshear's new-construction growth means a burst pipe is more often an installation issue than an age-related failure, but a fresh PEX line under pressure can fail just as fast as an old one. The same first steps apply either way.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source before anything else, if you know where your shutoff is located.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

Cut electricity at the breaker to any area with standing water before anyone walks through it.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the active leak or flooding.
