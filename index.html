Before You Call

What to Shut Off in a Plumbing Emergency

Find the Main Shutoff

Know where your home's main water valve is before an emergency happens, not during one.

Shut Off the Fixture First

A burst supply line under a sink can often be stopped at that fixture's own valve, not just the main.

Cut Power Near Standing Water

Turn off electricity to any area with active flooding before anyone walks through it.

Document for Insurance

Photograph the damage and the source before cleanup starts, in case you need to file a claim.
