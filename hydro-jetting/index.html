Hydro Jetting in Katy, TX

Snaked It and It Clogged Again a Few Weeks Later?

A drain snake clears the immediate blockage by cutting or pushing through it, but it doesn't remove what's built up along the pipe's interior walls, root growth, grease, or scale. That's exactly why the same line clogs again within weeks, and it's the specific problem hydro jetting is built to solve.

Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

Mature trees in Katy's older, established neighborhoods send roots into aging clay and cast iron sewer lines through even small cracks, and once inside, roots keep growing along the pipe's interior. Newer PVC lines resist this far better, but grease buildup in a kitchen line, old or new construction alike, produces the same recurring-clog pattern hydro jetting addresses.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Hydro Jetting

A Clog That Keeps Returning

The clearest sign something is built up along the pipe walls, not just blocking one spot.

Multiple Slow Drains at Once

Often points to a main line issue rather than an isolated fixture problem.

Root Intrusion Confirmed by Camera

A direct case for jetting over standard snaking.

Grease Buildup in a Kitchen Line

Common in both older and newer homes with heavy kitchen use.

Diagnosis

How Hydro Jetting Works

A plumber first runs a camera inspection to confirm the line's condition and the actual cause of the recurring clog, since jetting isn't the right tool for every situation. Once confirmed, a specialized nozzle sends high-pressure water through the line, scouring the full interior diameter rather than punching a single path through the blockage.

Options

Snaking or Hydro Jetting?

Snaking Handles

  • A single, isolated clog
  • Hair, soap buildup, minor blockages
  • A line with no history of recurring issues

Hydro Jetting Is Built For

  • A clog that keeps returning
  • Confirmed root intrusion
  • Heavy grease buildup along the pipe walls

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Avoid using chemical drain cleaners before the visit; they can complicate the actual diagnosis.

Note which fixtures are affected and how often the clog returns; it helps confirm whether jetting is the right fit.

If sewage is backing up, stay out of the affected area until it's addressed.

Don't attempt to run a home hydro-jetting attachment through an old or fragile line yourself; the pressure needed can damage a compromised pipe further.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

A plumber checks pipe condition first with a camera; a severely deteriorated line may need repair before jetting to avoid further damage.

How often does a line need hydro jetting?

It depends on the cause. A line with recurring root intrusion may need it every couple of years; a one-time grease buildup may not need it again.

Does hydro jetting damage residential pipes?

When matched to the pipe's actual condition, no. That's exactly why a camera inspection comes first.

Can hydro jetting be done on a kitchen sink line specifically?

Yes, it's a common and effective option for grease-related kitchen line clogs that keep returning.
