Housing Era

Older Cinco Ranch and the Newer Sections

The original Cinco Ranch sections east of the Grand Parkway were built starting in the 1990s, and homes there are old enough that original fixtures, water heaters, and supply lines are reaching the end of their practical life. Cinco Southwest and Cinco Northwest, built more recently on the community's west side, run modern PEX and code-compliant fixtures that are more likely to need a warranty-era repair than an age-related one. A plumber checks which era a home falls into before assuming what's likely wrong.

Neighborhoods

LaCenterra, the Golf Course, and Named Subdivisions

Cinco Ranch is large enough that it's really a collection of named subdivisions under one HOA umbrella, each with its own age and price point, sitting along the Cinco Ranch Golf Course and near the shopping and restaurants at LaCenterra. Some sections closer to the golf course and water features carry a higher water table, which is worth knowing before assuming a damp spot near a foundation wall is purely a plumbing leak rather than drainage.

Storm Season

Flood Zones and Gulf Coast Storm Patterns

Most of Cinco Ranch sits in Flood Zone X, outside the highest-risk flood designation, but a handful of subdivisions on the community's east side do carry 100- or 500-year flood zone status. That matters for sump pump reliability and backflow prevention during a heavy Gulf Coast storm, not just for homeowners insurance paperwork.

Given the split housing stock, the most common calls from Cinco Ranch are leak detection on the older side of the community and water heater or fixture warranty issues on the newer side. Utility Setup

Municipal Utility Districts, Not a Single City System

Like most of the Katy area, Cinco Ranch is served by a patchwork of Municipal Utility Districts (MUDs) rather than one citywide water utility, since Cinco Ranch itself is an unincorporated community inside Harris and Fort Bend counties. That's a normal setup for this part of Texas, and it doesn't change how a plumbing repair works, but it's worth knowing which MUD serves your section if a water quality or billing question comes up alongside a plumbing issue. Regardless of the MUD, all work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Texas, which sets the real standard for materials and installation, not just what gets a leak stopped fastest.

Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Cinco Ranch

Cinco Ranch's split housing stock means a burst pipe can come from either an aging original supply line on the east side or a fresh installation issue on the newer west side. Either way, the same first steps apply before help arrives.

Shut Off the Main Valve

Stop the water at its source before anything else, if you know where your shutoff is located.

Kill Power to Wet Areas

Cut electricity at the breaker to any area with standing water before anyone walks through it.

Clear the Immediate Area

Move people, pets, and valuables away from the active leak or flooding.
