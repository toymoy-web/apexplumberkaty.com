What This Page Covers

Katy Apex Plumbing ("we," "us," or "our") operates apexplumberkaty.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site and how it's used. We don't use contact forms on this site, so we don't collect information you type into one.

Information We Collect

What We Actually Gather

This site collects standard web analytics data automatically, such as pages visited, general location derived from IP address, browser type, and time on page, used to understand how the site is used and to improve it.
