Water Softener Installation & Repair in Katy, TX

A water softener removes the calcium and magnesium minerals responsible for hard water, extending the life of fixtures, water heaters, and appliances across the whole home. Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

West Houston's water carries enough mineral content that hard water is a real, common concern across Katy, showing up as scale buildup on fixtures, reduced water heater efficiency, and faster fixture wear than a softer-water market would see. A softener addresses this at the source rather than treating each symptom separately.

Signs to Watch For

Signs You Need Water Softener Installation & Repair

Scale Buildup on Fixtures

Chalky white residue around faucets and showerheads is the most visible sign.

Soap Not Lathering Well

Hard water reacts with soap differently, a common complaint in homes without a softener.

An Existing Softener Not Working

Often a salt or resin issue, worth diagnosing rather than assuming it needs replacement.

Faster Fixture Wear Than Expected

A sign hard water is accelerating wear on cartridges and valves.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose an Existing System

For a softener that's stopped working properly, a plumber checks the salt level and brine tank first, since that's the most common and simplest cause. From there, the resin bed and regeneration cycle get tested to confirm whether the system just needs maintenance or the resin itself has reached the end of its working life.

Options

Repair or Replace an Existing Softener?

Repair Usually Makes Sense When

  • The system is under 10 years old
  • The issue is salt, brine, or a control valve
  • This is the system's first real issue

Replacement Usually Makes Sense When

  • The resin bed has reached the end of its life
  • The system is 10-15+ years old
  • Repeated repairs haven't resolved the issue

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Check the salt level in the brine tank before assuming the system has failed; low salt is the most common cause of reduced performance.

Avoid using the wrong type of salt (check your system's manual); it can cause buildup issues.

Note whether water throughout the house feels hard, or just at one fixture, since that changes the likely cause.

Don't ignore a softener that's stopped regenerating; extended hard water exposure accelerates wear elsewhere in the home, including the water heater.

FAQs

Common Questions

How do I know if my softener just needs salt?

Check the brine tank level first. If it's low, refilling it is often all that's needed to restore normal function again.

How long do water softener systems last?

Typically 10 to 15 years with normal maintenance, though salt and regeneration settings need regular attention throughout.

Will a softener fix a chlorine taste in my water?

No, a softener addresses hardness, not taste. Filtration is the right fit for chlorine taste or odor concerns.

Is a salt-free softener alternative available?

Salt-free systems condition water differently and don't remove hardness the same way. A plumber can explain the full tradeoffs for your specific household needs.
