Buying a Home and Want to Know the Sewer Line's Real Condition?

A camera inspection is one of the few ways to actually see a sewer line's condition without digging, which makes it a genuinely useful step before buying an older Katy home, especially one with mature trees nearby that could mean root intrusion already in progress.

Local Factor

Why This Happens in Katy

Katy's mix of decades-old sewer lines in established neighborhoods and modern PVC in new construction means a camera inspection tells very different stories depending on the home's age and location. For older homes, it's often the only way to confirm whether a recurring clog is a simple blockage or a sign of deeper root intrusion.

Diagnosis

How the Inspection Works

A plumber feeds a waterproof camera on a flexible cable through an access point, usually a cleanout, and records real-time video as it travels the length of the line. The footage shows the pipe material, any cracks or root intrusion, and the exact distance to any problem area, which then informs whether a repair is even needed under the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standard Texas has adopted.

Options

Camera Inspection Alone or Paired With a Repair?

Inspection Alone Fits When

Pairing With Same-Visit Repair Fits When

Before We Arrive

What to Check or Shut Off

Have your cleanout location accessible before the visit if you know where it is; it speeds up the inspection.

Mention any recurring symptoms (odor, slow drains, past clogs) so the technician knows what to look for specifically.

Ask for the video footage or a summary of findings for your own records, especially if you're buying the home.

Don't skip this step before a major renovation involving the sewer line; confirming its condition upfront avoids a costly surprise mid-project.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can I get a copy of the inspection footage?

Yes, we can provide the footage or a summary of findings for your records.

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

Typically 30 to 60 minutes depending on the line's length and complexity.

Is this useful before buying an older Katy home?

Yes, it's one of the few ways to actually see the sewer line's real condition before closing on the purchase.

Can a camera inspection find a leak, not just a clog?

It can identify cracks and separated joints that may be leaking, though electronic leak detection is more precise for pinpointing an active leak location.
